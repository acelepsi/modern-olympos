Jury and Verdict

Jury & Final Verdict

Set the selected trial to Verdict to open voting. Check turnout before closing: no quorum or deadline is enforced. Closed is permanent; late votes are rejected. Publish once after closure using a server-verified ballot read. At least one valid ballot is required. Individual votes remain private.

Select a trial to check ballots.

Final tally publication is permanent. Review turnout before permanently closing in Trial Management.

Anonymous jury reasoning

Private ballots remain unchanged. After closure and tally publication, review each justification, redact personal information, then explicitly approve an anonymous public copy or exclude it. Public reasoning is a selected educational excerpt, not a second tally.

Encyclopedia

Teacher-authored research content only. Published entries are public: do not include student identities or private classroom information. Cite sources and distinguish differing traditions. Portrait URLs are validated without an administrator image preview.

Character research entry

Name, tradition and introduction are required. Published entries also require biography, actions, perspectives and sources. Use Draft to unpublish safely. Related trial IDs must reference existing trials.

Partner Schools

Only the administrator manages schools. Inactive schools remain assigned to existing accounts but do not appear publicly or receive new assignments. Schools cannot be deleted.
