Jury & Final Verdict

Set the selected trial to Verdict to open voting. Check turnout before closing: no quorum or deadline is enforced. Closed is permanent; late votes are rejected. Publish once after closure using a server-verified ballot read. At least one valid ballot is required. Individual votes remain private.

Anonymous jury reasoning

Private ballots remain unchanged. After closure and tally publication, review each justification, redact personal information, then explicitly approve an anonymous public copy or exclude it. Public reasoning is a selected educational excerpt, not a second tally.
