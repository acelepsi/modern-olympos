Who is Who?

Modern Olympos is a classroom courtroom for mythological figures. Read the case title in the active court and the teacher-approved arguments. Completed cases remain in the public archive.

Classroom roles

Prosecutor: presents the case against the mythological figure, with sources. Save a private draft or send your argument for teacher approval.

Defense representative: presents a supported defense. Returned work includes teacher feedback and can be edited and resubmitted.

Juror: reads the published arguments and casts one final vote with a reason when voting opens. Your individual ballot is private to you and the teacher; the final tally is public.

Observer: reads published content. A signed-in student with no assigned trial role cannot post or vote.

Teacher / administrator: creates student access, assigns roles, reviews arguments, opens voting, permanently closes cases and publishes final tallies. Teacher dashboard.

How a trial progresses

Trial: prepare arguments and receive feedback. Verdict: assigned jurors vote once. Closed: no further votes; the teacher publishes a tally after checking turnout. Closure and published votes cannot be undone.

Before you post

Use your teacher-assigned code and password. Keep passwords private, use your assigned display alias, cite your evidence and discuss mythological characters respectfully. Approved arguments and aliases are public: never include personal contact details or private student information.
