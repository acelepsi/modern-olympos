Modern Olympos

The Fictional Mythological Court

An international eTwinning project where students step into the court as Prosecutors, Lawyers, and Jurors to put figures of myth — Prometheus, Achilles, and others — on trial for the choices that defined them. Along the way, they build critical thinking, active citizenship, and language skills, arguing a case in a tongue and a tradition not their own.
